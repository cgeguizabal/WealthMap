## 2. What WealthMap is

WealthMap is a personal finance tracker operated by an individual developer
("the operator", "we"). You enter your accounts, cards, purchases, income and
goals, and it organises them and projects what you can safely spend.

## 3. What WealthMap is not

**It is not financial advice.** The figures WealthMap shows you — safe to spend,
liquidity projections, statement splits, interest estimates — are arithmetic
performed on numbers you typed in. They are not a recommendation, and they do
not account for anything you did not enter.

**It is not a bank, and it does not talk to your bank.** It holds no money,
moves no money, and has no connection to any financial institution. It cannot
pay a bill for you. Every balance in it is one you typed or corrected yourself.

**It is not a system of record.** Your bank's statement is authoritative;
WealthMap is your own notes about it.

Decisions about your money are yours. Before acting on anything WealthMap shows
you, check it against your actual accounts.

Bugs are possible. Report one and it will be looked at, but no warranty is made
that any figure is correct.
